Judgement only.

- Brand corporate summary pages (e.g. Ingka) are readable by fetch and give exact figures, but often lack a publication date and define scope vaguely. Read them first.
- Sustainability-report host sites (sustainabilityreports.com type) returned 403 to fetch. Don't spend a read on them.
- Search summaries give plausible figures (buyback counts, FSC %) that a primary read may not confirm. Keep them unconfirmed.
- Criticism sources are mostly NGO, documentary or blog. Label them as allegations.
- TikTok caption-limit guides are vendor blogs and conflict (2,200 vs 4,000). No official page surfaced.
- Next time: spend the second read on ikea.com climate agenda or an official TikTok help page, not a report host.
