# Caption writing

## Hook
The first line is what readers see before "more" (see `platform-specs`). Make it do one job. Four reliable kinds:
- a **specific fact** from the findings
- a **tension or question** the audience already feels
- a **concrete moment** (a scene, not a slogan)
- a **plain benefit** stated directly

Avoid "We're excited to announce", "Did you know", and openers that could start any post.

## Structure
Hook, then one idea, then one call to action. Short paragraphs. Ready to post as-is.

## Three options that really differ
Pick three **different angles**, for example benefit, story and curiosity. The test: cover everything except the first line of each option. If you can't tell them apart, rewrite until you can.

## Call to action
One verb, one next step, suited to the platform. On Instagram a caption link isn't tappable, so use "link in bio" or "comment".

## Tone
Take it from the brief. Match the voice, but keep the sentences short either way. If the brief gives a phrase to use or avoid, honor it.

## Facts
Use only facts that appear in the findings or the user's words (see `fact-grounding`). With empty findings, write from the brief and leave statistics out.

## Don't
- Stack hashtags (stay within `platform-specs`) or use a wall of emoji.
- Invent urgency ("last chance") nobody gave you.
- Offer three rewordings of the same line.
- Ask permission to write. Draft first and invite edits.
