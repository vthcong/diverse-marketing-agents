# Platform specs

Limits checked October 2026 against third-party guides, not official platform pages. Where sources disagreed it says so. If a platform isn't listed, say you don't have its specs. Don't guess.

The sources, the exact conflicting figures and what is still unchecked are in `reference.md` in this skill's folder. Read it only when you must quote a limit to the user or a cell below says unconfirmed.

| Platform | Hard limit | Visible before "more" | Hashtags | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | about 125, so the hook goes first | 3-5 relevant ones. Guides disagree on the cap (30 vs 5), unconfirmed | Links in captions aren't tappable, so use "link in bio", comment or DM |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | about 140 on mobile, 210 on desktop | at most 3, at the end | one clear action; short paragraphs |
| X | 280 characters (free accounts) | all of it | 1-2 at most | any link counts as 23 characters |
| Facebook | sources conflict (63,206 / 16,192 / 5,000), unconfirmed | not confirmed | few | keep posts short and front-load the point |
| TikTok | caption 2,200 or 4,000, sources conflict | not confirmed | a few | video length not checked |

## Rules for every platform
- The first line carries the hook, because it is often all a reader sees.
- One idea and one call to action per post.
- The hard limit is a ceiling, not a target.
- Check the format in the brief: feed post, Reel, Story, carousel or video.

## Video (HeyGen avatar)
- **50 words maximum** (`MAX_VIDEO_WORDS` in `heygen.py`, about 25 seconds at half a second a word). The tool rejects longer scripts.
- 720p only.
- Spoken text only: no hashtags, emoji, URLs or markdown. See `spoken-script`.
